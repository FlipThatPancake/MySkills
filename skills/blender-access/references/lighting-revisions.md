# Revise lighting

Read the light direction, shadow softness, and contrast from the reference.
Keep the camera, exposure, and view transform fixed for comparison unless one of them is the requested change.
For a local defect, inspect the surface and the light reaching or reflecting in it before changing global exposure.
Change one suspected cause at a time and use the result to choose the next adjustment.
For multiple looks, keep named lighting setups on shared geometry when practical.
