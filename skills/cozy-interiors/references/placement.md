# Placement

Place each thing where the person living here would use it: at the spot it serves, centred on what it belongs to, in reach from the seat or worktop. check-home measures the rest: its failures are rules, and its `[note-…]` lines are taste advice to weigh.

## What the checks cannot see
- A group of pictures reads as one piece, its outer edges on a shared rectangle.
- One rug per group.
- Lamps leave shelves in full view.
- The toaster stands by a socket, and a desk's screen at the back of the desk.
- Books stand upright with a few lying in stacks, objects among them, heavy things low.
- Heights step down from the tall ends of a group toward its middle.

Check: would the person living here put each thing there, and reach it from where they sit or stand?
