# Placement

Place each thing where the person living here would use it: at the spot it serves, centred on what it belongs to, in reach from the seat or worktop. Sizes in mm.

## Walls
- Over a sofa, bed, sideboard or console: one picture or a group about two thirds its width, centred on it, bottom edge 150–250 above it.
- A picture on open wall hangs with its middle at about 1500.
- A group of pictures reads as one piece: gaps 50–80, outer edges on a shared rectangle.
- Mirrors go where people stand or where they catch daylight: over a console, chest, basin or fireplace, in a hallway, full-length leaning in a bedroom or hall, or on a wall beside or facing a window. Over furniture a mirror is narrower than the piece below.
- Each wall piece has its own patch of wall, 100 or more from frames, shelves and other pieces.
- Hooks at about 1700, a bench or shoe rack under them.

## Seating
- Seats face each other or a focus (window, stove, coffee table) and turn their front or side to the viewer.
- Coffee table 400–450 from the sofa front, half to two thirds the sofa's length.
- Side table at a seat's arm, top about level with the arm.
- One rug per group, centred on it, under the front legs of every seat.
- Walkways 750–900 wide between large pieces.
- A sofa that backs onto a kitchen or table has a finished back or a console behind it, and a walkway clear behind that.

## Light
- Floor lamp beside or just behind the seat it lights, shade bottom near seated shoulder height (about 1050). Table lamp at a sofa end, on a nightstand, at one end of a console or a back corner of a desk.
- Pendant centred over the table or island it lights, bottom 750–900 above the top, about half the table's width.
- Lamps stand in open floor or wall space, leaving shelves, windows and pictures in full view.

## Kitchen
- Each thing lives at its work spot: pans, pot rail, utensil crock, oil and spices within about 1 m of the hob; dish rack, soap and tea towel at the sink; knives, boards and bowls on the prep stretch between sink and hob; kettle and toaster on free worktop by a socket.
- Free worktop beside each spot: 600 on one side of the sink and 400 on the other, 300–400 each side of the hob, 400 at the fridge's handle side.
- A pan stands on the hob and a bowl in the sink; everything else stands on worktop, shelf or rail.
- An island keeps 1000–1200 of free floor on each side where someone works or sits.

## Desk, reading, bed
- Desk side-on to the window or under it, daylight falling across the top; screen at the back, lamp at a back corner, chair tucked in.
- Reading chair by the window or with a lamp behind its shoulder, side table at hand, footstool in front.
- Bed headboard against the wall, centred; nightstands both sides, tops about level with the mattress; 600 free at the sides and foot.

## Tops and shelves
- Books stand upright with a few lying in stacks; objects among them; heavy things low.
- Small things rest wholly on their top, a finger's gap apart, in loose groups of one to three.

## Balance
- Tall pieces at the ends of a group and in corners, heights stepping down toward the middle.
- Visual weight on both sides of the view's centre.

Check: would the person living here put each thing there, and reach it from where they sit or stand?
