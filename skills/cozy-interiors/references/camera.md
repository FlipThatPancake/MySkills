# Camera

Every view is a composed photograph of one area, taken from inside it.

## Home view (`a.view`)
- Groups against the backing, open floor in front, hero near a third; the area fills the frame.
- Three layers: a foreground piece from the area's use, the main group, the wall with its opening.
- In an open-plan group, show a neighbour only at one side edge, a quarter of the width at most and clear of the subject.

## Tour shots (`a.shot`)
The idle page sways, then cuts between shots every 4 s; any input eases home. Three shots or more per area.
- A compact subject group, whole, in the middle third; a small foreground piece (under a tenth of the frame) cut at one edge; the area's things fill at least a third of the frame; background for depth.
- Leading lines: worktop edges, floorboards, runs.
- Vary height and lens: low across the floor, eye level along a worktop, high corner; fov 26–40.
- Window light and plants make good backgrounds.
- In an open-plan group a tour shot may show a neighbour behind its subject, for depth, never in front of it.
- A camera fitshots marks LOOSE fits only with looser framing; check-home fails it, so shrink the subject group or pick another subject.
