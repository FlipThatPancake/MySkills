# Look

Paper and pencil, lit softly. Calm and uncluttered, with a few signs of life.

- Fills shade by face direction: top #ffffff, front #f4f4f1, left #ededea, right #e5e5e2, underside #dcdcd9.
- Lines: 0.6 px, `mid` #8a8a8f for outlines, `lo` #b4b4b8 for small details, faint #c4c4c6 0.4 px for marks seen up close. Creases sharper than 30° drawn.
- Wall and floor melt into the backdrop #f4f4f1 with distance; seams fade out.
- Soft shadow under every object and anything lifted.
- Colour is rare: plants, daylight, one lit accent.
- Light on paper: a glowing part is a saturated body with a pale core; the cavity holding it gets its own darker ground. Tint pale tones; near-blacks stay neutral.
- Every line ends on something (an edge, a mount) or fades out.
- Every part rests on another part.
- 4× detail: up close, each visible area has something real (socket, switch, plinth gap, edge line).
- Motion eases in and out.
